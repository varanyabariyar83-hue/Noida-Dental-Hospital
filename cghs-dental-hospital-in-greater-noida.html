What Is CGHS and How Does It Work?

CGHS stands for the Central Government Health Scheme. It is run by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and gives medical care to central government employees, pensioners and their eligible family members. Beneficiaries are issued a CGHS card, which is the key to using the scheme.

Care is available through CGHS wellness centres and dispensaries, government hospitals, and private hospitals and diagnostic centres that are empanelled with the scheme. An empanelled hospital agrees to treat eligible beneficiaries at rates fixed by CGHS. For most beneficiaries this means treatment without paying at the counter, because the hospital bills CGHS directly. This is what people mean by cashless treatment.

That is why choosing a CGHS empanelled dental hospital in Greater Noida matters. If you go to a hospital that is not empanelled, you may have to pay yourself and claim a refund later, and only up to the approved CGHS rate. Going to an empanelled dentist avoids most of that trouble.

Who Is Eligible for CGHS Facilities?

CGHS is meant for people who have served or are connected with the Central Government. The main groups are listed below. The scheme is available in the cities where it has been extended, and eligibility depends on your category and your place of residence.

  • Serving central government employees paid from the Central Civil Estimates, with their eligible family members
  • Central government pensioners and family pensioners, with their eligible family members
  • Members of Parliament and former Members of Parliament
  • Sitting and former judges of the Supreme Court and certain High Courts
  • Former Governors, Lieutenant Governors and Vice Presidents
  • Employees of certain autonomous bodies and other organisations that have been brought under the scheme
  • Certain other categories notified by the Government from time to time, such as freedom fighters and accredited journalists in some cases

If you are not sure which category you belong to, your department, your pension office or your nearest CGHS wellness centre can confirm it. Once your card is issued, you can use it at any empanelled hospital, including with us. People searching for a CGHS dentist near me are usually cardholders who want a nearby empanelled hospital, and we are glad to help you check your eligibility before your visit.

Which Family Members Can Use the CGHS Card?

The card covers the beneficiary and the dependent family members named on it. In general, this includes the spouse, dependent parents (or parents-in-law in the case of a female employee), dependent children up to the age limit set by the scheme, and certain dependent daughters and sisters. Dependents other than the spouse must normally be living with the beneficiary and meet an income condition set by the scheme.

The exact definition of a dependent has been revised several times, so please check your own card. If a family member is named on your CGHS card, they can see our dentist on the same terms as you. Bring the card, a photo ID and any referral or permission letter your category requires.

CGHS for Beneficiaries Below 60 Years

Most people below 60 who use CGHS are serving central government employees and their family members, along with younger family pensioners and dependents. For this group the usual route is:

  • Visit your CGHS wellness centre first. For specialist consultation or treatment at a private empanelled hospital, the wellness centre medical officer normally gives you a referral.
  • Serving employees who need planned treatment at an empanelled hospital usually need a permission letter from their department before treatment starts, so that the hospital can bill CGHS directly. Without it, you may have to pay and claim reimbursement.
  • Emergencies are different. In an emergency, you can go straight to an empanelled hospital and no prior permission is needed.

So if you are under 60 and looking for CGHS dental clinic in Greater Noida, plan ahead: get your referral or permission letter, carry your card and ID, and call us to book a time. We will tell you what is needed for your particular treatment.

CGHS for Beneficiaries Between 60 and 70 Years

Most people in this age group are pensioners. Pensioners with a valid CGHS card are entitled to cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals, and the hospital should not ask you for an advance deposit for covered treatment.

For this group, a referral from the CGHS wellness centre is usually needed to consult a specialist or to begin treatment at a private empanelled hospital. A referral is generally valid for a limited period and a limited number of consultations, so check the dates on your slip. Planned tests and procedures may need their own referral or endorsement.

If you are between 60 and 70 and want to see a dentist, bring your card, photo ID and referral slip. We will examine you, explain the treatment plan, and tell you what falls within the CGHS list before we begin. As a CGHS approved dentist in Greater Noida, we want you to understand every step and every document before you are treated.

CGHS for Beneficiaries Above 70 Years

Senior citizens get an important relaxation. Under a notification issued by the CGHS in August 2024, CGHS beneficiaries aged 70 years and above can consult specialists at empanelled hospitals directly in the outpatient department, without first taking a referral from the CGHS wellness centre. You still need your valid CGHS card and photo ID.

This makes life much easier for older patients, because you can book an appointment and walk in for an opinion on your teeth, dentures or gums without going to the wellness centre first. Treatment that follows the consultation is still subject to the usual CGHS rules and rates, so we explain what is covered before we start.

Emergencies, as for every age group, do not need a permission letter. Please note that age-based relaxations have changed over the years and may change again, so confirm the current rule with the CGHS portal or your wellness centre. If you are above 70 and searching for a CGHS dental hospital near me, call us and we will walk you through your visit.

What Does a CGHS Approved Dentist Mean?

Patients often say CGHS approved, but the correct term is empanelled. A hospital is empanelled when it has been through the CGHS process, agreed to the scheme rates and terms, and been added to the official list of empanelled hospitals for that city. A CGHS approved dentist in Greater Noida is therefore a hospital that is on that list and is allowed to treat cardholders cashless.

Before you go to any hospital, you can ask to see its empanelment details, and you can also check the list on the official CGHS website or ask your wellness centre. Being transparent about this is part of earning your trust.
