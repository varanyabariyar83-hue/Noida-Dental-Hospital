What to Expect at Your First Visit

  • You tell us why you came and what worries you.
  • We examine your teeth, gums and bite.
  • X-rays or photographs are taken only when they are needed.
  • We explain what we found and show you where possible.
  • We discuss the options, the expected timeline and the cost.
  • You choose what to do, and we plan the next visit.

A first visit often takes less time than people expect. If you are anxious, tell us and we will slow down. Many patients are surprised by how comfortable it is.
