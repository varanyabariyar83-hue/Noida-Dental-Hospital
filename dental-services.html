General and Preventive Dentistry

Prevention is the cheapest and most comfortable kind of dental care. A check-up and clean every six months lets us catch cavities, gum problems and worn fillings early, while they are small. We also help you set up a home routine that works for you, with advice on brushing, cleaning between teeth and diet.

Fillings repair decay, and our approach is to remove as little of the natural tooth as possible. Toothache and emergencies are seen as quickly as we can, with pain relief first and a clear plan to follow.

Restorative Dentistry: Root Canals, Crowns, Bridges and Implants

When a tooth is badly damaged, we aim to save it. Root canal treatment removes infection and lets you keep your tooth, and a crown protects it afterwards. If a tooth cannot be saved, crowns, bridges and dental implants replace it. Each has its place, and we explain the benefits and limits of each before you choose.

Wisdom teeth that cause pain or infection are removed carefully, with a plan for aftercare. Our treatment pages explain every step so that you know what to expect.

How to Choose the Right Treatment

How Treatment Works at Noida Dental Hospital

Every treatment follows the same simple path. We begin with an examination and, if needed, X-rays. We then explain what we found and the options in plain language. You see the estimate before treatment begins. We carry out the treatment at your pace, with local anaesthesia where needed, and finish with clear aftercare advice and a review.

This approach applies to everything from a simple filling to a complex plan with several stages. It keeps you informed, and it avoids surprises. For longer plans, we break the work into stages so you can see what is next and decide at each step.

Preparing for Treatment

Most treatments need little preparation. For anything involving anaesthesia, eat lightly beforehand. Tell us about your medical history, any medicines you take and any allergies. If you take blood thinners or have a heart condition, diabetes or are pregnant, let us know, because it affects how we plan treatment.

For longer plans, such as braces, implants or crowns, we ask you to bring any old records and X-rays. We also ask whether you have any fears or past bad experiences, so we can plan around them.
