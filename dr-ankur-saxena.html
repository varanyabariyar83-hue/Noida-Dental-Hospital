About Dr. Ankur Saxena

Dr. Ankur Saxena is the dentist at Noida Dental Hospital. He sees patients of all ages and believes in careful diagnosis, plain explanations and treatment that respects your time and comfort. His approach is simple: listen first, examine carefully, explain the options and let you decide.

How a Consultation Works

  • We listen to your concerns and what you want to change.
  • We examine your teeth, gums and bite.
  • X-rays or photographs are taken where needed.
  • You see and hear what we found, in plain language.
  • We explain the options, the expected timeline and an estimate.
  • You decide in your own time, and there is no pressure.

If your case calls for a specialist opinion, we will say so. We would rather refer you than provide treatment that is not right for you.

Approach to Care

Dr. Saxena’s approach rests on three ideas. First, diagnose before treating: an accurate diagnosis saves time and cost, and avoids unnecessary treatment. Second, explain before acting: you should understand what is planned and why. Third, treat gently: comfort is part of good care, not a luxury.

For longer treatments, he plans in stages and checks progress at each. He is happy for you to ask questions and to take time to decide. If a treatment is not needed, he will say so.
