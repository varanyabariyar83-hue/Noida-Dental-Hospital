Privacy Policy

Noida Dental Hospital respects your privacy. This page explains how we handle information you share with us through this website.

Information we collect

If you use the appointment form, your name, phone number and appointment preferences are placed into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send to us. We do not store this information on this website.

How we use it

We use the details you send only to respond to you and arrange your appointment and care.

Third parties

WhatsApp, Google Fonts and Google Maps (used to show our location) are third-party services with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information.
